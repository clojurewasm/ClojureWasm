;; `conj!` on a TRANSIENT map accepts everything persistent `conj` on a map
;; accepts: nil is a no-op and another map merges entry by entry. Both raised
;; IllegalArgumentException until 2026-09-10, which also broke
;; `(into (transient {}) {…})`. The persistent half is conj_map_merge.txt; the
;; persistent side had been taught the rule and the transient twin was missed.
;;
;; Note the deliberate asymmetry pinned below: `(conj! (transient []) nil)` is
;; [nil], because a vector conj's nil as an ELEMENT, while the map treats nil
;; as nothing to add. A set likewise keeps #{nil}.
;;
;; NOT covered here: a non-nil, non-map, non-[k v] entry (e.g. '(:a 1)). cljw
;; raises IllegalArgumentException where clj raises ClassCastException; that is
;; the pre-existing D-459 choice, unchanged by this fix.
(persistent! (conj! (transient {}) nil))
;;=> {}
(persistent! (conj! (transient {}) {}))
;;=> {}
(persistent! (conj! (transient {}) {:a 1}))
;;=> {:a 1}
(persistent! (conj! (transient {:z 0}) {:a 1 :b 2}))
;;=> {:z 0, :a 1, :b 2}
(persistent! (conj! (transient {:a 1}) {:a 9}))
;;=> {:a 9}
(persistent! (conj! (transient {}) [:a 1]))
;;=> {:a 1}
(persistent! (conj! (transient {}) (first {:x 9})))
;;=> {:x 9}
(persistent! (conj! (conj! (transient {}) {:a 1}) {:b 2}))
;;=> {:a 1, :b 2}
(count (persistent! (conj! (transient {}) {:a 1 :b 2 :c 3})))
;;=> 3
(persistent! (conj! (transient []) nil))
;;=> [nil]
(persistent! (conj! (transient #{}) nil))
;;=> #{nil}
(persistent! (reduce conj! (transient {}) [{:a 1} {:b 2} nil]))
;;=> {:a 1, :b 2}
